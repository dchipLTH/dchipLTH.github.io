PAINT & SIP with Ericsson! 🖌️

Welcome to an inspiring evening where creativity meets networking! Ericsson invites all members of Elektra and D-chip to Måla & Skåla – an event where we’ll decorate beautiful glasses, mingle, and enjoy delicious food and refreshing drinks. Let your creativity flow, make new connections, and be inspired by great conversations.

This is a fantastic opportunity to meet Ericsson employees in a relaxed setting. Take the chance to ask questions, exchange ideas, and gain insight into what it’s like to work at one of the world’s leading technology companies. Whether you’re curious about career opportunities, technology, or simply looking for a fun and rewarding evening, you’re more than welcome to join us!
