💖 Congratulations on your admission and a warm welcome to the D-Section and D-Chip, the association for women and non-binary students at the D-Section! 💖

As tradition goes, we invite all new and returning members to our welcome lunch the day before the semester starts.

We'll meet outside the E-House (keep an eye out for our pink overalls! 💗), where we'll start with some fun activities before heading down to the D-Section's premises, iDét, located in the basement of the E-House. There will be lunch, a quiz, and a perfect opportunity to get to know both new and returning D-Chip members!

The event is completely free, but registration is required! ✨

Hope to see you there!

Lots of love, D-Chip 💖
